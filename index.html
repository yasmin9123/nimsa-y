The NIMSA-Y flow

From parts to launch.

Instead of one giant page, each phase gets its own focused workspace.

01 — DESIGN

Build the rocket

Drag in a nose cone, body tube, fins, motor and recovery system. Enter exact dimensions, masses and materials.

02 — CONDITIONS

Describe the launch

Enter temperature, wind, pressure, humidity and launch angle so the analysis can account for launch-day conditions.

03 — ANALYZE

Generate the flight

Produce estimated apogee, velocity, acceleration, burnout, flight time, drift and synchronized flight graphs.

Built for iteration.

Import a CAD file or build directly in the studio. Every design stays in your browser while you move between pages.
